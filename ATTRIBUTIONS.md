## Libraries shipped with the app

These are bundled into the built client or run on the server.

| --- | --- | --- |

| [three-mesh-bvh](https://github.com/gkjohnson/three-mesh-bvh) | MIT | Fast picking on detailed models |

occt-import-js is used unmodified. Its files and both licence texts are copied from the npm package into `client/public/occt/` when the client is built, and are served next to the app as `/occt/license.occt-import-js.txt` and `/occt/license.occt.txt`. To use a different build of the library, replace the files in that folder.

## Bundled boards

The breadboards and the Arduino, Raspberry Pi, STM32, FTDI and CH340G boards in the library are simplified models generated by this project's own code (`shared/src/builtin/`). No manufacturer 3D files are included.

Arduino, Raspberry Pi, STM32, FTDI and the other product names are trademarks of their owners. They are used here only to say which board a model stands for.
